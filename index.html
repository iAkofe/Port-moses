Customer Settings

Termii Group · CPaaS
Termii Engage
0 to MVP · AI-Powered Customer Engagement for Africa
Overview
Businesses use tools like Customer.io to automate messages and engage their customers across different stages of a journey: onboarding nudges, churn recovery, upsell flows, and more. Termii Engage is that platform, built specifically for African businesses. But what makes it fundamentally different is how it thinks about who a customer is.
The Problem
Every major competitor in this space (Customer.io, Braze, Klaviyo) defines a customer by their email address. That is the primary key. Bunce, where I worked previously, took a small step forward by allowing either email or phone number. But in Nigeria and across much of Africa, this still falls short.

After rigorous research with banks and large microfinance banks, we uncovered a deeper truth: data is a fundamental problem. A highly informal economy, a largely unbanked population, and low digital literacy mean that many customers of financial institutions simply do not have email addresses on file. Some never will. Building a customer engagement platform on email as the foundation was building on sand.

The implication was serious: these businesses could not use any existing tool to properly identify, track, or engage their own customers at scale.
The Innovation
We rethought the foundation. Rather than imposing a primary key on businesses, we gave them the power to decide. Any attribute in their customer data (phone number, BVN, account number, customer ID) could be designated as the primary key, as long as it held a unique value per customer.

This was not a small UX tweak. It was a architectural decision that changed what was possible. A bank could now say "our primary identifier is account number." An MFB could say "use BVN." A retail business could say "use loyalty card ID." Each business defined their own truth about who their customers are, and Engage was built to respect that.

On top of this, we infused AI across the platform: AI-generated copy for message campaigns, automatic customer segmentation based on behavioural patterns, pattern discovery across customer data, and AI-suggested engagement strategies, so businesses are not just reacting to churn but anticipating it.

AltSchool Africa · Education
AI Product Manager Course
4-Week Curriculum · AI Product Management
Overview
I designed and facilitate a 4-week AI Product Management course at AltSchool Africa, one of Africa's leading tech education platforms. The course is built for beginner Product Managers who want to work at the frontier of product and AI, not just learn about it from the sidelines.
The Problem
Most Product Management education treats AI as a topic to understand rather than a tool to wield. The result is a generation of product managers who can describe what AI does but cannot use it to move faster, make better decisions, or ship more effectively than their peers. In a market where AI is rapidly compressing the time it takes to go from idea to product, that gap is career-defining.
The Approach
The curriculum is built around a single north star: raise PMs who can build AI products and move faster and more effectively than their peers because they are AI-powered.

The course is structured around the Anthropic 4D Framework (Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence) as a backbone thread across all four weeks. Modules cover AI Literacy, the AI Product Manager Role, the Framework in Practice, and a final "Ship It" capstone. Every assessment is designed as a single-effort, dual-output artifact: a LinkedIn post that feeds directly into the Week 4 capstone, where students build and deploy a live product prototype.

The focus is not just on understanding AI but on using it daily as a Product Manager: for research synthesis, spec writing, prioritisation, user interviews, and rapid prototyping. Students leave with a shipped product, a public portfolio, and the muscle memory to work AI-first.

Bunce
Developer Experience · Event Tracking · AI Content Generation
Overview
At Bunce, I served as Product Manager with a focus on developer experience. I owned and managed the developer portal (developers.bunce.so), ensuring that the technical documentation, onboarding flows, and integration experience were clear, complete, and developer-friendly. Event Tracking Feature
I led the development of the event tracking feature — a capability that lets businesses track activities happening within their applications and use those events to trigger automated engagement flows. When a user completes an action (a payment, a sign-up, a drop-off), Bunce captures that event and fires the right message at the right time. This was a foundational feature that made Bunce's automation engine meaningfully intelligent rather than time-based.

Arca Payments · FinTech · B2B
Arca Payments
Card Management Portal · B2B Card Issuance Infrastructure
Overview
Arca Payments is a card issuance infrastructure provider operating in the Nigerian fintech space. The model is B2B: Arca issues cards to fintech companies, and those fintechs in turn issue cards to their own customers. My role was to build product capabilities that gave these fintech businesses the tools they needed to properly manage the cards they had issued on behalf of their customers.
The Problem
Fintech companies using Arca's card issuance infrastructure had no self-service way to manage the cards they issued to their customers. If one of their customers needed a card cancelled, a spend limit adjusted, or a card frozen due to suspected fraud, the fintech had to route the request through Arca's support team. This created a slow, manual, multi-party process for something that should have been instant. It slowed down the fintechs, frustrated their customers, and put unnecessary load on Arca's operations.
The Innovation
I designed and led the development of a self-service Card Management Portal that gave fintech businesses direct control over the cards they had issued. Through the portal, a fintech could cancel a customer's card, freeze and unfreeze cards, configure spend limits, and manage card-level controls, all in real time without involving Arca's support team.

This shifted the operating model from reactive and manual to self-service and instant. I wrote the full PRD, worked through the security and compliance requirements with engineering, and managed stakeholder sign-off across product and leadership.
